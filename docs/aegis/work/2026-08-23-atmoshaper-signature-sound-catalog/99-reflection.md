# AtmoShaper Signature Sound Catalog Reflection

## Original catalog and processing work

- The conservative production declaration correctly remained separate from broad discovery leads.
- Independent review pressure strengthened schema closure, evidence semantics, physical output confinement, and immutable publication history before handoff.
- The original four-list report was accurate for declared production candidates but not sufficient as a claim about everything present in the downloaded library.

## Exhaustive discovery and listening curation

- The exhaustive review corrected the declaration-only blind spot by accounting for every scanned recording and exposing obvious filename-level sources such as snow and stone footsteps.
- Keeping browser draft state disposable was appropriate for auditioning, but the returned export required a separate fingerprint-bound import owner before it could become durable project evidence.
- Source decisions and group decisions must remain independent. An explicit Reject removes one recording; it does not deactivate the concept or imply that composition work should stop.
- Assembly requirements belong in structured playback strategies. Whole-source shuffling, adaptive one-shot sequencing, walking cadence, and spaced events express active design work without qualifying or publishing the underlying recordings.
- Recording review and group review need separate browser state and fingerprints. This lets a rejected ingredient stay excluded while the group behavior is independently approved, changed, or annotated.

## Boundary and follow-up

- The curation slice intentionally stops before production dynamic scheduling, exact cadence or transition calibration, technical qualification, processing, media publication, and deployment.
- The next handoff is the user's fingerprinted group-review export. Reconcile that export into the human-owned strategy declaration before a runtime slice consumes the contract; the later runtime slice should verify scheduling, transitions, cancellation, gain safety, and reduced-resource behavior in the browser.
- Development review endpoints and the importer should be retired when the local listening workflow is no longer needed; the fingerprinted curation remains the durable decision record.
