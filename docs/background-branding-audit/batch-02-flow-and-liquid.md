# Background Branding Audit: Flow and liquid motion

## Tidal Glass
- **ID:** `massage-lab-wave-current`
- **Current name:** Wave Current
- **Decision:** rename
- **Alternatives:** Subsurface Cells; Glass Tide
- **Visual descriptor:** Layered liquid contours moving with directional flow
- **Signature original eligible:** No
- **Rationale:** Tidal Glass captures the glossy shader surface and its smooth directional movement while resolving the catalog's duplicate Wave Current label.
- **Collision notes:** Judge its broad liquid cells against Line Current's fine contours and Ribbon Tide's blurred bands, not against the similarly named legacy entry.

## Line Current
- **ID:** `massage-lab-waves`
- **Current name:** Wave Current
- **Decision:** rename
- **Alternatives:** Fine Topography; Parallel Murmur
- **Visual descriptor:** Fine parallel lines undulating as an ordered field
- **Signature original eligible:** No
- **Rationale:** Line Current resolves the duplicate current name by foregrounding the source-shaped canvas strands and their coordinated wave motion.
- **Collision notes:** Its many narrow parallel paths distinguish it from Filament Waves, Floating Lines, and Tidal Glass's broad liquid cells.

## Ribbon Tide
- **ID:** `massage-lab-wavy-background`
- **Current name:** Wave flow
- **Decision:** rename
- **Alternatives:** Velvet Roll; Soft Lull
- **Visual descriptor:** Blurred ribbons rolling across an open field
- **Signature original eligible:** No
- **Rationale:** Ribbon Tide gives the soft canvas waves a tactile, restorative identity that reflects their wide blurred bands rather than a generic flow label.
- **Collision notes:** Its few broad blurred ribbons contrast with Floating Lines' layered arcs and Line Current's dense ordered contours.

## Silk
- **ID:** `massage-lab-silk`
- **Current name:** Silk
- **Decision:** keep
- **Alternatives:** Fabric Fold; Luminous Undulation
- **Visual descriptor:** Silk-like folds rippling through a field
- **Signature original eligible:** No
- **Rationale:** Silk is short, sensory, and exact for the shader's fabric-like folds, making the established source name unusually well aligned with the desired voice.
- **Collision notes:** The textile surface separates Silk from Iridescence's reflective wash and Prismatic Ribbons' discrete bands.

## Floating Lines
- **ID:** `massage-lab-floating-lines`
- **Current name:** Floating Lines
- **Decision:** keep
- **Alternatives:** Suspended Lines; Arc Current
- **Visual descriptor:** Layered lines sweeping in arcs
- **Signature original eligible:** No
- **Rationale:** Floating Lines plainly names the multiple elevated bands and their slow suspended travel without adding a stronger energy cue than the default shows.
- **Collision notes:** Layered arcs distinguish this renderer from the single fine Threads field and Filament Waves' dense bundle.

## Filament Waves
- **ID:** `massage-lab-line-waves`
- **Current name:** Line Waves
- **Decision:** rename
- **Alternatives:** Luminous Weave; Fine Harmonics
- **Visual descriptor:** Dense filaments folding like waves
- **Signature original eligible:** No
- **Rationale:** Filament Waves is more tactile and specific than the existing Line Waves phrase, matching the dense bundle of many hair-thin shader lines.
- **Collision notes:** Density is the key separator from Threads' sparse strands, Line Current's evenly spaced contours, and Floating Lines' layered bands.

## Threads
- **ID:** `massage-lab-threads`
- **Current name:** Threads
- **Decision:** keep
- **Alternatives:** Wandering Threads; Delicate Weave
- **Visual descriptor:** Fine threads wandering through a field
- **Signature original eligible:** No
- **Rationale:** Threads is compact and truthful to the restrained Perlin line field, whose delicate paths need no more elaborate promise.
- **Collision notes:** Use the sparse thread count to keep this title distinct from Filament Waves' thick bundle and Line Current's ordered sheet.

## Prismatic Ribbons
- **ID:** `massage-lab-color-bends`
- **Current name:** Color Bends
- **Decision:** rename
- **Alternatives:** Band Fold; Ribbon Transit
- **Visual descriptor:** Separated bands bending across a field
- **Signature original eligible:** No
- **Rationale:** Prismatic Ribbons replaces an awkward verb phrase with a pronounceable image that describes the shader's separated bands and flexible curvature.
- **Collision notes:** The continuous bending bands differ from Prismatic Burst's radial rays, Prism's triangle, and Iridescence's blended wash.

## Liquid Ether
- **ID:** `massage-lab-liquid-ether`
- **Current name:** Liquid Ether
- **Decision:** keep
- **Alternatives:** Fluid Infusion; Weightless Plume
- **Visual descriptor:** Fluid plumes curling through a translucent volume
- **Signature original eligible:** No
- **Rationale:** Liquid Ether is memorable and lightly experimental while accurately evoking the simulation's weightless plumes and translucent volume.
- **Collision notes:** Its diffuse plume behavior is separate from Molten Mirror's reflective spiral and Ferrofluid's outlined cellular contours.

## Molten Mirror
- **ID:** `massage-lab-liquid-chrome`
- **Current name:** Chrome Flow
- **Decision:** rename
- **Alternatives:** Mirror Whirlpool; Reflective Bloom
- **Visual descriptor:** Reflective liquid spiraling into a central void
- **Signature original eligible:** No
- **Rationale:** Molten Mirror describes the high-contrast reflective whirl directly and avoids colliding with the other catalog entry currently called Chrome Flow.
- **Collision notes:** Reserve this name for the central reflective spiral; Chrome Marbling describes the broader flow shader in Batch 6.

## Ferrofluid
- **ID:** `massage-lab-ferrofluid`
- **Current name:** Ferrofluid
- **Decision:** keep
- **Alternatives:** Magnetic Bloom; Fluid Filaments
- **Visual descriptor:** Liquid contours clustering and separating
- **Signature original eligible:** No
- **Rationale:** Ferrofluid is a precise, pronounceable material reference for the rim-lit cellular forms and preserves a strong source name without a medical implication.
- **Collision notes:** Outlined droplet contours distinguish it from Liquid Ether's cloudy plumes, Plasma's rounded cells, and the mirrorlike chrome effects.

## Iridescence
- **ID:** `massage-lab-iridescence`
- **Current name:** Iridescence
- **Decision:** keep
- **Alternatives:** Soapfilm Light; Opaline Drift
- **Visual descriptor:** Reflective light folding across a fabric-like surface
- **Signature original eligible:** No
- **Rationale:** Iridescence already expresses the renderer's defining color-shifting behavior and gives the soft shader wash a calm, sensory, single-word title.
- **Collision notes:** Its blended reflective surface is distinct from Silk's textile folds, a grainy banded field, and Aurora Wash's vertical curtains.
