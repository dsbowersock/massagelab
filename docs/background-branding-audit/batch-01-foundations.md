# Background Branding Audit: Foundations and signature forms

## Massage Laba Lamp
- **ID:** `massage-lab-moving-gradient`
- **Current name:** MassageLaba Lamp
- **Decision:** rename
- **Alternatives:** Orbital Ember; Restorative Colorfield
- **Visual descriptor:** Warm color orbs drifting through a dark field
- **Signature original eligible:** Yes
- **Rationale:** The spaced signature treatment makes the internally conceived free default easier to pronounce while preserving its familiar lamp identity.
- **Collision notes:** Reserve this sole branded title for the original moving gradient; Lamp Glow and Colorfield Drift describe separate premium compositions.

## Still Gradient
- **ID:** `static-gradient`
- **Current name:** Static gradient
- **Decision:** rename
- **Alternatives:** Held Light; Amber Blue Haze
- **Visual descriptor:** Fixed amber and blue gradient haze
- **Signature original eligible:** Yes
- **Rationale:** Still Gradient plainly identifies the motionless internal fallback and gives it a calm, intentional role rather than sounding like a technical state.
- **Collision notes:** The word Still separates this fixed field from the moving lamp, Colorfield Drift, and Color Drift recommendations.

## Retro Grid
- **ID:** `massage-lab-retro-grid`
- **Current name:** Retro Grid
- **Decision:** keep
- **Alternatives:** Neon Vanishing Point; Endless Perspective
- **Visual descriptor:** Perspective grid scrolling toward the horizon
- **Signature original eligible:** No
- **Rationale:** Retro Grid is concise, immediately recognizable, and truthful to the receding wire plane without overpromising a futuristic experience.
- **Collision notes:** Its horizon scroll distinguishes it from Grid Scan's tunnel, Ripple Grid's center wave, and the flat tile systems.

## Aerial Rays
- **ID:** `massage-lab-aerial-rays`
- **Current name:** Aerial Rays
- **Decision:** keep
- **Alternatives:** Skybreak; Softlight Descent
- **Visual descriptor:** Diffuse light rays descending from above
- **Signature original eligible:** No
- **Rationale:** Aerial Rays already carries a calm sense of height and accurately describes the passive top-down shafts seen at the default settings.
- **Collision notes:** The overhead diffuse origin keeps it apart from Lateral Rays, Cascading Rays, Spotlight, and the centered lamp effects.

## DNA
- **ID:** `massage-lab-dna`
- **Current name:** DNA
- **Decision:** keep
- **Alternatives:** Helix Rhythm; Genetic Geometry
- **Visual descriptor:** Colorful double helices rotating in depth
- **Signature original eligible:** No
- **Rationale:** DNA is the most truthful and scannable name for the reviewed paired-base helix, while remaining descriptive rather than making a health claim.
- **Collision notes:** The biological base-pair structure is unique; Twisted Cubes also rotates in depth but has no organic or educational form.

## Twisted Cubes

## Speckled Veil
- **ID:** `massage-lab-reveal-dots`
- **Current name:** Reveal dots
- **Decision:** rename
- **Alternatives:** Luminous Pinfield; Quiet Pointillism
- **Visual descriptor:** Tiny cyan dots shimmering across darkness
